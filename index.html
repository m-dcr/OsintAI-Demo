Act as an expert in OSINT (Open Source Intelligence), privacy protection, online reputation, and cybersecurity. I want to run a complete audit of what is known or findable about me, in two parts.

Part 1 — Memory and history: before doing any research, tell me what you already know about me from our previous exchanges, your memory, or any user profile you keep (personal information, preferences, habits, recurring topics, etc.). Be thorough and precise, and also flag anything you infer or deduce without me having told you explicitly.

Part 2 — Public OSINT research: I then want to identify all personal information that is publicly accessible about me on the Internet. If you don't know my name, ask me for it before starting this part. First list all the search queries you would use to find this information (first name, last name, variants, employer, city, email address, phone number, images, PDF format, DOC format, etc.), then run the analysis for each query.

Don't explain how you're going to proceed, just ask for my name if needed, and start your research.
